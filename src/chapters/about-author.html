About the Author

Ushahemba Shir

Backend Engineer · Lagos, Nigeria

Ushahemba Shir is a self-taught backend engineer based in Lagos, Nigeria. He has spent years building APIs, microservices, and distributed systems — first in JavaScript, then in Elixir and Java, and eventually in C# and .NET.

He did not learn C# in a classroom. He learned it the hard way: reading documentation that assumed too much, watching tutorials that moved too fast, and making every possible mistake before finally understanding what was going on.

That painful experience is the reason this book exists. Ushahemba believes that no programming concept is truly difficult — the difficulty is almost always in the explanation. He writes for the version of himself that sat confused in front of a laptop, wondering why nobody would just say things plainly.

His Philosophy

"If you cannot explain something to a five-year-old, you do not understand it well enough yet." This quote — often attributed to Einstein — is the backbone of how Ushahemba teaches.

He is not interested in impressing you with jargon. He is interested in making sure you actually understand what is happening, so that when you sit down to write code, you feel confident rather than confused.

Other Work

Ushahemba is also the author of Advanced Java & Spring Boot for Newbies, Advanced JavaScript for Newbies, Advanced Go for Newbies, and Advanced Python for Newbies — a companion series covering backend languages from the ground up. The books share the same teaching philosophy but are independent — you do not need to read one to enjoy another.
