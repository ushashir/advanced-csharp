About This Book

C# has a reputation for being complicated. People say things like "C# is only for Windows" or "ASP.NET Core is too much magic." Both of those things can feel true when you are starting out.

But here is the secret: C# is not complicated. The explanations of C# are often complicated. This book exists to fix that.

Every concept in this book — from classes and objects to JWT authentication — is explained through a simple story or analogy first. The code comes second. By the time you see the code, you already understand what it is supposed to do.

This is the book I wish had existed when I started learning C#. I hope it makes the journey easier for you.

Who Is This Book For?

  • Complete beginners who have never written a line of C#
  • Developers who know another language and want to learn C# and .NET
  • Students who found their C# textbook confusing
  • Anyone who has tried to learn ASP.NET Core and felt lost in the scaffolding

How to Use This Book

Read it from beginning to end for the full journey, or jump to any chapter that interests you. Each chapter builds on earlier ones, but every chapter also contains enough context to stand alone.

If you are completely new to programming, start at Chapter 1. If you already know basic C#, jump to Part 2 or Part 3.
