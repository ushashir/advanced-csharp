About This Book

C# has a reputation for being complicated. People say things like "C# is only for Windows" or "ASP.NET Core is too much magic." Both of those things can feel true when you are starting out.

But here is the secret: C# is not complicated. The explanations of C# are often complicated. This book exists to fix that.

Every concept — from data types and data structures to JWT authentication — is explained through a simple story or analogy first. The code comes second. By the time you see the code, you already understand what it is supposed to do.
